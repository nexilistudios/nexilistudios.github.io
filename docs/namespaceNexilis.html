enum Nexilis.PacketTransport

The transport a raw package is sent over.

Enumerator
Tcp 

Reliable, ordered, connection based. Use for room and player management packets and for anything that must not get lost.

Udp 

Unreliable, unordered, datagram based. Use for latency sensitive data such as positions, where a dropped packet is cheaper than a retransmit.
