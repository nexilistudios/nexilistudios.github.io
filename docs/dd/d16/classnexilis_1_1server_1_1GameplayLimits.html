Detailed Description

Gameplay limits the server enforces on player actions. Every limit has a default and is configurable through the server's ServerConfig. Direct client positions outside the world bounds are rejected; movement results, speeds, and damage outside their limits are clamped. Shots that arrive faster than the shooting rate allows are dropped. Broadcasts that exceed the message length or rate limits are rejected.
